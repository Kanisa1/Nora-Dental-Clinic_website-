01About Nora Dental Clinic

Caring for Your Smile,
One Patient at a Time.

Welcome to Nora Dental Clinic, located in Kigali, Gasabo, Rwanda. We are dedicated to supporting better oral health by providing dental care in a welcoming, respectful and patient-centred environment.

We believe that every patient deserves attention, clear communication and care that considers their individual needs. Our aim is to make every visit a positive experience while encouraging healthy dental habits and long-term oral well-being.

02Who we are

A dental clinic for individuals and families.

Nora Dental Clinic is a dental healthcare facility committed to serving individuals and families through professional dental care and guidance.

We understand that visiting a dental clinic can sometimes feel uncomfortable. That is why we aim to create an environment where patients feel welcomed, listened to and informed about their dental care.

03Our purpose

Our Mission & Vision

Our mission

Patient-centred care, with compassion.

To provide accessible, patient-centred dental care through professional service, compassion and a commitment to improving oral health and well-being.

Our vision

A trusted dental care provider.

To become a trusted dental care provider recognised for quality service, patient satisfaction and a positive contribution to community oral health.
